# Sources and attribution

Consulted September 2026. Explanations are original summaries and remain drafts.

- Noble RJ, Hillis JS, Rothbaum DA. Electrocardiography. In: Clinical Methods, 3rd edition, Chapter 33 (1990). https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK354/ — ECG definition, electrical activation, P/QRS/T and PR definitions. Used for foundational physiology, not contemporary treatment recommendations.
- Merck Manual Professional Edition. Atrioventricular Block. https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/cardiovascular-disorders/specific-cardiac-arrhythmias/atrioventricular-block — prolonged AV conduction and classic Mobitz I sequence.

The sinus marker offset, four-cycle structure, waveform functions and numerical parameters are declared design choices for teaching. No claim is made that they were validated by these sources. Sources are linked, not relicensed. No source illustrations or recordings are bundled.
