# Clinical review

Current status: **Draft — clinician review pending** for every lesson.

Record the reviewer's name and relevant credentials with permission, date, exact commit/version, scope, sources, issues and resolution. A review of educational accuracy is not clinical validation.

## Checklist

- [ ] Patient right/left orientation, chamber labels and conduction structures are clear.
- [ ] P, PR, QRS, ST and T explanations correctly distinguish electrical and mechanical events.
- [ ] PR is not equated with AV nodal delay alone.
- [ ] The normal conduction order and all guided anchors are consistent.
- [ ] Prolonged PR preserves one-to-one atrioventricular conduction.
- [ ] Wenckebach has progressively longer PR intervals before a nonconducted P wave and coherent reset.
- [ ] No QRS or T is assigned to the nonconducted impulse.
- [ ] Numerical teaching parameters and limitations are appropriate and visible.
- [ ] Questions have supported answers and unambiguous explanations.
- [ ] Sources support the associated content and are accessible.
- [ ] No patient data, treatment advice or diagnostic claims are introduced.

Do not change the draft badge automatically. Update the type/content and review log only after the project owner confirms completed review. Re-review affected content after substantive model changes.
